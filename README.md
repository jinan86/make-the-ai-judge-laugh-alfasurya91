Entry for the [Make the AI Judge Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alfasurya91-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-6ed40225) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one joke about office life, one line, under 200 characters, judged head-to-head by an AI comedy-club headliner.

## The joke

> The fire drill is the only meeting where everyone shows up on time, leaves together, and nobody asks for the recording.

## Why this one

The field (162 submissions) is saturated with printer jams, "per my last email", reply-all, meeting-could-have-been-an-email, standups, coffee machines, HR "we're a family", spreadsheets, office plants, out-of-office replies and IT tickets. A fire drill appears in none of them — yet it is a universal office ritual, and the twist lands on the one thing every real meeting produces that nobody wants: the recording.

## How it works

`main.py` is deterministic: no randomness, no network, no clock. It writes the single-line joke (119 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
